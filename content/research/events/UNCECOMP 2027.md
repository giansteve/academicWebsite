- **What:**
	- **MS11: From Sensitivity to Knowledge: Global Sensitivity Analysis as a Framework for Information Gain and Loss in Computational Models** - F. Schäfer, G.M. Melito, S. Brandstäter, S. Razavi, B. Iooss
  Global sensitivity analysis is usually presented as a diagnostic tool. This minisymposium takes a different starting point: the major sensitivity measures, from Sobol indices to DGSM, HSIC and the Borgonovo indices, can be read as special cases of a single information-theoretic quantity, each resting on its own distributional assumptions. Framed this way, sensitivity analysis answers three connected questions: how much information an input carries about the output, how much information a proposed experiment provides about the parameters, and how much information is lost when a high-fidelity model is replaced by a surrogate or when a parameter is fixed. Contributions are invited on mutual-information and HSIC-based indices, expected information gain and Bayesian optimal experimental design, the Fisher information and DGSM-Sobol connection, information loss from surrogate truncation, Shapley-value decompositions, and sensitivity-guided Bayesian inversion.
