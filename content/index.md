Hey, I am Assistant Professor at the [Institute of Mechanics](https://www.tugraz.at/institute/ifm/home) at [Graz University of Technology](https://www.tugraz.at/en/home).

My research focuses on **uncertainty quantification** and **sensitivity analysis** of computational mechanics. The majority of my work had a strong focus in the mechanics and modeling of **[[aortic dissection]]** during my PhD. Currently, I am deepening into other fields where uncertainty is of particular interest. 

Uncertainty and stochasticity are everywhere around us, from the tools we use every day, to the natural and artificial phenomena happening around us. Although this can feel overwhelming, I find there is a fascinating side in it. 

Teaching is my duty at the university, but also something I love doing. Working with students, helping them grow academically and professionally is nice to experience to see in their path. I am currently teaching the exercise section of the Master's course in **Multibody Dynamics** (Mechanical Engineering program) and supporting the Bachelor's courses **Mechanik - Statik** and **Mechanik - Dynamik** for the Biomedical Engineering program.
